About Me

My academic background, interests and goals

I am a motivated Electrical and Electronic Engineering student at American International University-Bangladesh, with a strong foundation in electrical circuits, electronic systems, power systems and technical problem-solving.

My practical interests are in power and backup equipment such as UPS, VFD, battery chargers and battery systems, as well as solar system design. I also enjoy building small electronics projects, and I am an active member of the AIUB Robotics Crew.

I am looking for an entry-level position where I can apply my engineering knowledge, develop practical skills, and contribute to innovative and efficient engineering solutions while growing professionally.
