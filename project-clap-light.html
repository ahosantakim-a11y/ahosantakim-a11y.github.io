Clap-Controlled Light System

A lighting system that turns a light on or off in response to hand claps, built with sound detection and electronic switching.

ElectronicsSensorsCircuit design

Overview

What I built and my role

Goal

Control a light without touching a switch: a hand clap turns the light on, and the next clap turns it off.

My role

I developed the system, including the sound detection and the electronic switching that controls the light.

How It Works

From a clap to a light

1

Detect the sound

The circuit listens for a sudden loud sound, a hand clap.

2

Switch the light

Electronic switching turns the light on or off each time a clap is detected.
